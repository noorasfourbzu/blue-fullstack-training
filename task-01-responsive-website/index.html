About Us

AsfouraBandora is an independent game studio built for curious players and unusual ideas.

We create puzzle-driven experiences where playful worlds meet strange rules, psychological themes, dark comedy, and meaningful questions.

Our games invite players to explore, experiment, take risks, and look at ordinary things from unexpected angles.

What Lives in Our Games?

  • Strange little worlds
  • Psychological ideas
  • Dark and playful humor
  • Hidden philosophical questions
  • Risks worth taking
